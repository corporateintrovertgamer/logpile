Built for Collectors

Everything You Own, Instantly.

Launchers today exist to push sales and microtransactions. Logpile exists purely to celebrate the games you already have.

🗂️

Automatic Multi-Storefront Discovery

Seamlessly indexes installed titles across Steam, Epic Games, GOG, EA, Ubisoft, and standalone installs. Everything is organized into dedicated workspace filters without running heavy background storefronts.

🔒

Local-First SQLite

Your playtime, favorites, and metadata live in a clean SQLite database on your local disk. No accounts, no cloud sync timeouts, and zero analytics tracking. Everything keeps working offline once your artwork is fetched.

🖼️

Automated Artwork Scraper

Replaces missing covers with official high-res vertical posters, custom hero backdrops, and clear title logos through SteamGridDB.

⚡

Zero-Bloat Hardware Performance

Built with hardware-accelerated rendering and list virtualization. Logpile handles libraries of 2,000+ titles with silky 60+ FPS navigation and a lean idle footprint.

Good to Know

Questions, Answered.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. Your library lives in a local SQLite database, so browsing and launching work offline. A connection is only needed to download artwork from SteamGridDB.

Which launchers does Logpile support?

Steam, Epic Games, GOG, EA, Ubisoft, and standalone games, all indexed into one library.

Is it really free?

Yes. Logpile is free and open source under the MIT License, and the source is public on GitHub.

Where is my data stored?

On your own disk in a local SQLite database. There is no cloud sync and no analytics tracking.

Which platforms are supported?

Logpile v1.0 is a Windows desktop app.

What is coming next?

Five layout modes (Cinematic, Kinetic, Atmospheric, Playful and Competitive) are planned for v1.1. See the roadmap above.
