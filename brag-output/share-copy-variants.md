## Reddit (r/pcgaming / r/IndieGaming / r/emulation)
**Title:** I got tired of launchers acting like slow digital shopping malls, so I built Logpile — an ad-free, 4K game library manager.

**Body:**
Modern storefronts take 15–30 seconds to boot and shove store banners down your throat. I built **Logpile** as an open-source, local-first sanctuary for collectors:
- **Instant Cold Boot:** Sub-second startup backed by local SQLite.
- **Three Layout Archetypes:** Cinematic (wide theatrical hero art), Kinetic (neon esports pulse), and Atmospheric (Nordic frosted acrylic minimalism).
- **Auto Artwork Scraping:** Plugs into SteamGridDB & IGDB for vertical covers, clear logos, and hero banners.
- **Series & Studio Intelligence:** Auto-links sequels, prequels, and developer history.
- **Zero Ads & Zero Telemetry:** Your data stays on your machine.
